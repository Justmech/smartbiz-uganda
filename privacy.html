Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

SmartBiz Uganda is currently a local-first prototype for recording business sales, products, stock and operating expenses.

What information is stored?

Information you enter (such as product names, quantities, sale dates, customer names you choose to enter, and expense details) is stored in your browser's local storage on the device and browser you use. This prototype has no account system, cloud synchronisation, analytics, advertising SDK, or built-in payment processing.

Who receives the information?

This prototype does not intentionally transmit the records you enter to the app owner. If you export a CSV or JSON backup, the file is saved through your browser and you control where you share it. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access locally stored records.

Backups and deletion

Use the app's Backup JSON feature to keep a separate backup. Clearing browser site data, uninstalling a browser, or changing devices may remove access to locally stored records. You can delete individual records in the app; this prototype does not yet include a full account-level data deletion workflow.

Children and sensitive data

This app is designed for business recordkeeping, not for storing passwords, bank credentials, national identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information.

Future changes

Before adding accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, or payments, this notice must be reviewed and updated to describe the actual services, data collected, retention, security, and contact details. This notice is a prototype disclosure and not legal advice.

Contact

There is no dedicated support email configured in this prototype yet. A public release should add a verified support contact before launch.
